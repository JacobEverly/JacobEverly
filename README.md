# Jacob Everly

Technical product leader working on model customization, post-training, and inference.

I lead an open-model inference platform at [Boundless](https://boundless.network). Before that I took its distributed compute marketplace from concept to market (150+ providers, 10,000+ GPUs at peak, 1.9M jobs). Earlier: product at Ava Labs and systems engineering at Raytheon. I studied mechanical engineering as an undergraduate at Oklahoma State, then did my master's at Cornell Tech focused on machine learning.

I also train and evaluate open models on multi-step, tool-using tasks. I'm especially interested in coordinating agent swarms and in AI for cybersecurity.

## Selected work

- **[fantasy-alpha](https://github.com/JacobEverly/fantasy-alpha)**: Qwen3.5-9B LoRA fine-tuning on Tinker for multi-step, tool-using decisions. Training loss fell from 1.257 to 0.815, but complete episodes did not improve, so I stopped before RL. Frozen evaluations, negative results, and exact cost accounting are included.
- **[long-horizon-supervisor](https://github.com/JacobEverly/long-horizon-supervisor)**: a verifier-gated portfolio of models raised coding-agent completion from 7/18 to 12/18 at slightly lower cost than the best single model. Includes 715 public agent trajectories.
- **[always-on-agents](https://github.com/JacobEverly/always-on-agents)**: the product layer built on fantasy-alpha, an agent that advises a human during a draft without taking control.
- **[prover-killers](https://github.com/JacobEverly/prover-killers)**: worst-case benchmarks for zero-knowledge prover cost.
